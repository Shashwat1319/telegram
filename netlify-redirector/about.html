About SmartGahr

SmartGahr is an Indian deals website that finds genuine Amazon deals — with one strict rule: only products with 40% or more real discount make the cut. No spam, no fake "limited time" tricks, only price drops worth your attention.

What we do

  • Track thousands of Amazon India products daily for real price drops
  • Verify every deal — genuine MRP, genuine discount, genuine rating
  • Publish only the best deals on our website and Telegram channel
  • Keep budget picks easy to find with an under-₹999 filter on the deals page

How we choose deals

Every product passes a filter before it appears here:

  • Discount must be 40%+ against a real MRP (inflated-MRP games are rejected)
  • Rating must be 4.0+ stars from real buyers
  • Price range: ₹149 budget picks to premium gear — we show the real price, never a "from ₹999" trick
  • Product must be from a trusted seller with good reviews
