Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SmartGahr ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit our website at smartgahr.netlify.app (the "Site").

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account or log in. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment information.

  • Analytics data: We use privacy-friendly analytics to understand page views and deal clicks in aggregate (e.g., which deals are popular). This data is anonymized and cannot identify you personally.
  • Click tracking: When you click a deal link, we record the click (date, product, and referral source) to measure which deals interest our audience and to attribute affiliate commissions.
  • Cookies: We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on our own pages. Third-party services (Amazon, Telegram) may set their own cookies when you visit them through our links, governed by their own policies.

Our deal links take you to Amazon.in (and occasionally other retailers). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

4. Advertising

We may display advertisements on this site (for example, Google AdSense). Ad networks may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Children's privacy

Our site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
