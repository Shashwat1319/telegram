Privacy Policy

Kitchen Finds India — effective October 27, 2026

Who We Are

Kitchen Finds India ("the App") is a content optimization and publishing platform for Pinterest creators. For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@kitchenfindsindia.com.

Information We Collect

  • Pinterest account data (via OAuth): only the permissions you explicitly approve when authorizing the App (currently: create/update boards, create/update pins, read your user account profile). We do not request or store your Pinterest password.
  • Technical data: standard server logs such as browser type, approximate location (IP-based), pages visited, and cookies or local storage needed to keep your session working.
  • Contact data: your email address, only if you provide it when contacting support.

How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App (publishing, scheduling, and optimization features).
  • To authenticate you securely with Pinterest through their official OAuth flow.
  • To respond to support requests and diagnose technical problems.

Sharing and Disclosure

  • Pinterest: data is sent to Pinterest only as required to perform the actions you authorize through the official Pinterest API.
  • Service providers: hosting and infrastructure providers (e.g., Netlify) process data solely to run the App.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  • We may disclose data if required by law.

Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing support@kitchenfindsindia.com. You can also revoke the App's access from your Pinterest account settings at any time.

Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data (email us).
  • Disconnect the App from Pinterest at any time (Pinterest → Settings → Authorized apps).
  • Opt out of non-essential cookies via your browser settings.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures (HTTPS, token-based authentication, no storage of Pinterest passwords) to protect your information. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
