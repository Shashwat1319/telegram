# Phase 2: Channel Swap — DM Template

## How to Find Swap Partners
1. Search Telegram for "deals", "amazon deals", "home deals india"
2. Find channels with 50-500 members (not too big, not too small)
3. DM the admin (check channel bio for contact)

---

## DM Template (Copy-Paste)

> Hey! Love your channel. I run @smartgahr — an automated Amazon deals channel (Home & Kitchen, 50-80% off).
>
> Want to do a mutual mention? I'll pin a post about your channel for 24h, you do the same. No strings attached.
>
> Let me know!

---

## Alternative: Shorter Version

> Hi! I run @smartgahr (deals channel). Want to swap a mention? I'll pin yours, you pin mine. Let me know!

---

## After They Agree

1. Prepare a short, attractive post about their channel
2. Pin it in @smartgahr for 24 hours
3. Ask them to do the same
4. DM 5-10 channel admins daily
